# Model Procurement Language

*Informative. Buyers may adapt this language. It binds no one until it is in a contract.*

## RFP requirement

> **Proof of Performance.** As a condition of evaluation, the Vendor shall participate in a Proof of Performance Engagement under PP-SPEC-079 (The Coal Mine Protocol), v0.1.0 or later, in [In-Situ Mode in the Buyer's pre-production environment | Independent Lab Mode at a laboratory of the Buyer's choosing], using Mine Plan version [X] for technology profile(s) [077 Section 17 profiles]. The Vendor shall submit a Claim Sheet under PP-SPEC-079 Section 8.1 covering every efficacy, containment, and operational claim in its proposal and public materials. The Vendor acknowledges that every committed run will be published to a PP-SPEC-004 registry regardless of outcome, that the Vendor will occupy no Integration Point, and that Vendor-operated testing will not be accepted as Proof of Performance. The Buyer will fund the Engagement [or: the Engagement is Vendor-Funded and will be labeled as such].

## Contract clause

> **Continuous Proof of Performance.** For the term, the Product shall operate under a PP-SPEC-077 Production Stream commitment in the Buyer's production environment, with the Proof Witness operated by [Buyer's security operations | named auditor]. A Claim Finding (PP-SPEC-077 11.6) against any claim on the Vendor's Claim Sheet, or a Performance Specification failure recorded under PP-SPEC-013 in the same series, constitutes [a service credit of X | a material breach after Y findings in Z days]. An undeclared DUT Fingerprint change (PP-SPEC-077 15.4.3) is a change-control violation under Section [N]. The Vendor retains its Right of Reply under PP-SPEC-079 Section 8.4.

## Evaluation scoring note

> Performance Records will be compared only within the same Comparability Class (PP-SPEC-079 Section 9.5). Records from different Environment Classes or Mine Plan versions will not be placed in the same table.

## What a vendor can say afterward

> "Product X, version Y, was tested under PP-SPEC-079 in [mode], Comparability Class [C]. The Performance Record is at [Registry URI]." Any number quoted must appear in that record.
